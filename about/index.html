About Yakton Tools

Yakton Tools is a collection of small utilities designed to help with common text, image, coding, design, and calculation tasks. The project prioritizes dedicated tool pages, clear instructions, and local processing wherever feasible.

Design principles

  • No account required for ordinary tools.
  • Be transparent about when data leaves the browser.
  • Do not invent usage statistics, reviews, or rankings.
  • Keep pages accessible and responsive.
