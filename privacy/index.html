Privacy

Most tools process content in your browser. This site does not intentionally upload text or image files to Yakton servers, and generated passwords are not stored by the site.

Third-party QR service

The QR code generator sends the entered text to api.qrserver.com to render a QR image. Do not submit confidential information through that tool.

Hosting logs

Cloudflare may process standard request metadata as part of delivering the website. No analytics script is included by default.
