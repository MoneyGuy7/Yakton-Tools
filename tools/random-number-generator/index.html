About the random number generator

Pick fair random numbers for games, raffles and sampling. Uses the browser’s secure random generator with bias-free selection.

Frequently asked questions

Are the numbers truly random?

They come from the browser’s cryptographically secure generator, which is far stronger than Math.random for fairness.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
