Frequently asked questions

Is the QR code generator private?

No — the text you enter is sent to api.qrserver.com to draw the QR image, so avoid anything confidential. Most other tools on this site run fully offline.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?

The QR text is sent to a third-party image service (api.qrserver.com). Avoid private data.
