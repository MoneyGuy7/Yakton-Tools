About the url encoder / decoder

Encode special characters for query strings or decode messy percent-encoded links. Choose component encoding for parameters or full-URL encoding for whole addresses.

Frequently asked questions

Component vs full URL encoding?

Component encoding escapes characters like / ? & = so a value is safe inside a parameter; full-URL encoding leaves those structural characters alone.

Is this tool free to use?

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
