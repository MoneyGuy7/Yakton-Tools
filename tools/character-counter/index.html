About the character counter

Check the exact length of tweets, SMS messages, page titles and meta descriptions. The counter shows characters with and without whitespace, along with words, sentences and paragraphs.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good meta description length?

Search engines typically show about 150–160 characters of a meta description, and around 50–60 characters of a page title.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
