About the remove extra spaces

Fix text copied from PDFs, emails or web pages that has doubled spaces, stray tabs and empty lines. You can also join everything into a single clean line.

Frequently asked questions

Does it remove tabs and non-breaking spaces?

Yes — tabs and non-breaking spaces are treated as whitespace and collapsed to a single normal space.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
