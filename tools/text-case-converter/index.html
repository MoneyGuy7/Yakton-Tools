Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?

camelCase starts with a lowercase letter (myVariable) while PascalCase capitalises every word including the first (MyVariable).

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
