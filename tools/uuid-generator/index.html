Frequently asked questions

Are UUIDs really unique?

Version 4 UUIDs have 122 random bits, so the chance of a collision is astronomically small for any practical use.

Is this tool free to use?

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
