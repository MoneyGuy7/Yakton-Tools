About the hash generator (sha-256, sha-512)

Compute cryptographic hashes using your browser’s Web Crypto API. Useful for checksums and verifying that two pieces of text are identical.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use SHA-256 to store passwords?

Not on its own. Passwords need a slow, salted algorithm such as bcrypt, scrypt or Argon2 on a server.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
