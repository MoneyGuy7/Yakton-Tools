About the pomodoro timer

Use the Pomodoro technique to stay focused: work for 25 minutes, take a short break, and a longer one after several rounds.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Pomodoro technique?

A time-management method of working in focused 25-minute blocks separated by short breaks, with a longer break after about four blocks.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?
