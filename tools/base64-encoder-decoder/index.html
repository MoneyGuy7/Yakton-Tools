About the base64 encoder / decoder

Encode or decode Base64 strings used in data URIs, tokens and APIs. Unicode text such as emoji and accents is handled correctly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is only an encoding that makes data text-safe; anyone can decode it, so never use it to protect secrets.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, sign-up or watermark.

Does my data leave my device?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser; your input is not uploaded to Yakton servers.
